AI Automation Workspace

A personal workspace for exploring how artificial intelligence can support documentation, analysis and small repeatable workflows.

What this is

  • An evolving personal laboratory.
  • A place to document prompts, constraints and validation steps.
  • A way to compare manual and AI-assisted workflows.

Boundaries

  • Experiments use non-confidential material.
  • AI-generated outputs require human review.
  • The workspace is not presented as a production-ready platform.
